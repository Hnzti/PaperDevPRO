Privacy Policy

PaperDeveloper
Effective date: July 23, 2026

Overview

PaperDeveloper is an offline darkroom timer app. It does not require an account and does not use a cloud service for app features.

Data collection

PaperDeveloper does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Settings, presets and chemical usage information are stored only on your device (local storage / UserDefaults).

Tracking

PaperDeveloper does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Third parties

PaperDeveloper does not sell your data and does not share app data with third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data.
